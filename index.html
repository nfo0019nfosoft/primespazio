DESIGN INSPIRATIONS

10 Most Popular Interior Design Styles

Every interior design style has its unique characteristics. By understanding more, they'll become easier to identify and recreate perfectly for your space.

01

Minimalism

Focused on simplicity and function. Utilizes minimal furniture and clean neutral colors to enhance open luxury spaces.

02

Contemporary

Features clean lines, forms, and unique structural shapes. Often incorporates highly progressive modern materials and elements.

03

Industrial

Uses raw structural elements such as exposed brick and metal beams. Has a sophisticated urban warehouse-inspired look.

04

Mid-Century Modern

Combines sleek architectural lines with organic flow shapes. Highly popular worldwide for its signature retro-vintage appeal.

05

Transitional

Blends traditional core heritage values beautifully with contemporary layout profiles. Achieves a well-balanced timeless look.

06

Scandinavian

Combines organic light natural colors and bright textures. Emphasizes a strong sense of coziness and sleek functional minimalism.

07

Bohemian

Uses eclectic, artistic and completely unconventional layout decor. Has a rich global-inspired, deeply relaxed free vibe.

08

Modern Farmhouse

Combines rustic traditional structural logs with modern flat design profiles to create a warm, inviting living atmosphere.

09

Rustic

Emphasizes organic natural elements and beautifully weathered raw materials. Features a rugged, heritage and highly cozy aesthetic.

10

Traditional

Inspired directly by elegant 18th and 19th-century styles. Incorporates timeless classic patterns and high-order decor structures.
